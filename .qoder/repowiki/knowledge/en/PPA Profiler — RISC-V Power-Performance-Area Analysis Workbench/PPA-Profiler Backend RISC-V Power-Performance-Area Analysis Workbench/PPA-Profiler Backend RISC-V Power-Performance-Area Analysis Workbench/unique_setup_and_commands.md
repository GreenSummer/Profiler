CLI entry point `ppa.cli` exposes `ppa init` (create DB), `ppa gen-sample <dir>` (generate synthetic RTLA/PrimePower/SPECint runs), `ppa ingest <run_dir>` (parse manifest and load), `ppa demo` (generate+ingest in one step), and `ppa serve [host] [port]` (uvicorn). All settings are overridden via `PPA_*` environment variables (e.g. `PPA_DB_PATH`, `PPA_AI_BASE_URL`, `PPA_AI_MODEL`, `PPA_FRONTEND_DIST`).
